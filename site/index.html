Every agent at a glance.

Shepherd is not another terminal multiplexer. herdr keeps running your sessions and agents; Shepherd is the window onto them, drawn with GPUI and its own terminal.

agents

Who needs you

The agents list shows every agent herdr detects in any workspace, with its status: working, blocked, done or idle, the task in its title and how long it has waited. Click one to jump to its pane.

notifications

Approve from the notification

When an agent you can't see starts waiting or finishes, macOS shows a notification and the Dock icon counts it until you look. Approve on the notification answers the agent without opening Shepherd.

worktrees

Worktrees under the repo

The sidebar folds a repo's worktrees under it with their branch, changes against main and PR state. Right-click creates, opens or removes one.

panes

Tabs and panes you can drag

Drag tabs to reorder them. Drag a pane by its grip onto another pane to split or swap, or onto a tab to move it there. Dividers snap to herdr's cell grid and the terminal reflows while you drag.

terminal

Its own terminal

libghostty-vt with Shepherd's glyph renderer: ligatures, Kitty images and box drawing. Scrollback is read from herdr once and scrolled locally. ⌘-click opens links, files, folders and images.

review

The PR without leaving

⌥⌘B opens the review panel: the branch's PR (gh) or MR (glab) with its description, checks or pipeline jobs and their logs. It works for SSH machines too.

Keys you already know.

⌘K opens a command panel with every action, workspace and tab. The status bar shows the branch, its PR and CI, the current folder, the focused agent and your Claude and Codex plan limits.

Sessions and saved SSH machines sit in a bar under the sidebar, as Arc's spaces; a two-finger swipe goes to the next one. Settings cover fonts, themes, key bindings and usage, and edit herdr's own config.toml keeping your comments. Every binding can be changed.

iPhone · Android

Your agents, in your pocket.

Shepherd for iPhone and Android shows the same herdr session away from the desk: every agent with its status, a push when one is blocked or done, and the Live Activity on the lock screen. Tap the notification and it opens that agent's pane.

It is an SSH and mosh terminal too, so it reaches any server, with herdr or without.

On your phone

Shepherd for iPhone and Android watches the same herdr session from your pocket: agent status on the lock screen, a push when one needs you, one tap to answer. It is also an SSH and mosh terminal, so it reaches the server with or without herdr. More about it.

Needs herdr

Shepherd talks to the herdr server over ~/.config/herdr/herdr.sock, so herdr has to be installed and running. You can point it at another binary or socket in Settings.
